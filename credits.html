Dialogues, phrases and exercises

Written for BaMeLe (tagged written in the content files), modelled on wording that is widely attested in everyday Malaysian usage. No native-speaker review has been done, so treat the wording as a learner's aid, not an authority.

Module 1 contains no copied sentences. Module 5 is built mainly from sentences copied unchanged from Tatoeba; in the app they carry a “Real sentence” mark. English translations and everything else were written for BaMeLe.
