Credits & sources

BaMaLe is a personal study tool for everyday spoken Malaysian Malay. It hosts only material that may be republished; everything else is linked or embedded.

Dialogues, phrases and exercises

Written for BaMaLe (tagged written in the content files), modelled on wording that is widely attested in everyday Malaysian usage. No native-speaker review has been done, so every line carries a “Feels off?” flag for later correction.

No sentences have been copied from third-party corpora in the current content. If Tatoeba sentences are added, each will be listed here with its author under CC BY 2.0 FR.

Audio

“Learning voice” audio is synthesised with Microsoft's Malaysian Malay neural voices (ms-MY Osman and Yasmin) and stored as MP3 files. These voices are clean and fairly standard; they pronounce short forms more carefully than real speakers do.

Real clips

“Real Malay” clips are YouTube embeds cued to a start and end time. Nothing is downloaded or rehosted; all rights remain with the creators. Candidate clips for Module 1:
