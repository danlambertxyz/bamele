BaMeLe is a personal study tool for everyday spoken Malaysian Malay. It hosts only material that may be republished; everything else is linked or embedded.

Dialogues, phrases and exercises

Written for BaMeLe (tagged written in the content files), modelled on wording that is widely attested in everyday Malaysian usage. No native-speaker review has been done, so treat the wording as a learner's aid, not an authority.

Module 1 contains no copied sentences. Module 5 is built mainly from sentences copied unchanged from Tatoeba; in the app they carry a “Real sentence” mark. English translations and everything else were written for BaMeLe.

Audio

“Learning voice” audio is synthesised with Microsoft's Malaysian Malay neural voices (ms-MY Osman and Yasmin) and stored as MP3 files. These voices are clean and fairly standard; they pronounce short forms more carefully than real speakers do.

Real clips

“Real Malay” clips are YouTube embeds cued to a start and end time. Nothing is downloaded or rehosted; all rights remain with the creators. Candidate clips for Module 1:
